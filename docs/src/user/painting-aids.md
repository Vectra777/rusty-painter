# Shapes and painting aids

## Shapes

`U`: line, rectangle, ellipse, polygon and Bézier curve, drawn with the brush, filled, or both, and editable until applied. For the curve, click for corners and drag for smooth points, then drag the anchors and handles afterwards; Alt breaks a handle pair.

**QuickShape**: hold the pen still at the end of a stroke and it becomes a clean line, ellipse (turned the way it was drawn), rectangle or polygon, editable before it's applied.

## Pen

`Shift+P`: Bézier paths, as Photoshop's Pen. The document has one work path, any number of lines, kept with the project.

- **Draw**: click for a corner, drag for a smooth point (Alt while dragging breaks its handles, for a corner into the next curve). Click the line's last point again to take back its out handle. Click the first point to close the shape. Enter or Esc ends the line; Backspace takes back its last point. Shift keeps a new segment to 45°.
- **On the path**, with no line being drawn: click a curve to add a point (the curve keeps its shape), click a point to delete it, click an open line's end to carry it on.
- **Edit** (Ctrl held, or the Edit mode): drag a point, a handle (Alt: on its own) or a curve.
- **Convert** (Alt, or the Convert mode): click a point to turn it from corner to smooth and back.
- **Use it**: Make Selection (`Ctrl+Enter`, or add, subtract, intersect), Fill with the brush colour, Stroke with the brush (on a vector layer, as lines), Clear.

While the Pen is chosen, `Ctrl+Z` steps back through path edits first.

## Ruler and assistants

`R`: a ruler that straightens strokes along or parallel to it. Vanishing-point, perspective, ellipse and concentric assistants (View → Assistants, or the Shape menu) snap strokes to them.

## Grid and guides

- View → Grid (`Ctrl+'`): square or isometric, with a pixel grid from 800%.
- Guides (`Ctrl+;`): Ctrl+drag from beside the canvas, or View → Guides. Ctrl+drag a guide to move it. Guides are saved with the project.
- Snap to Guides and Snap to Grid.

## Mirror painting

Left/right, top/bottom, four-way or radial (mandala, optionally kaleidoscope) around a centre and angle you drag on the canvas. `H` flips the view.

## Wrap Around

View → Wrap Around paints across the canvas's edges and shows it repeated, for seamless tiles.

## Reference and navigator

A Reference Image window to pick colours from, and a Navigator showing the whole canvas.
