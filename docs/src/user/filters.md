# Filters

Every filter previews live on the layer or the selection; sliders preview smoothly even on a whole 4K canvas. The colour adjustments are also available as [adjustment layers](layers.md#adjustment-layers), and the blur tool's Filter mode paints any filter through the brush.

| Kind | Filters |
|---|---|
| Colour | Brightness/contrast, hue/saturation, levels, curves (a master curve and one per channel), colour balance (shadows, midtones, highlights), gradient map (any colours, with presets), invert, desaturate, posterize, threshold, exposure, temperature/tint, vibrance, sepia, solarize |
| Blur and sharpen | Gaussian, motion, zoom and spin blur, sharpen, reduce noise (median) |
| Effects | Glow, chromatic aberration, halftone, emboss, find edges, oil paint, vignette, noise, pixelate, dither, clouds |
| Line art | Extract Line Art: a scanned drawing's paper turns transparent |

## Gradients

`Shift+G`: linear, radial, reflected, angle and diamond gradients, previewed live, dithered, clipped to the selection. Drag the ends again before applying; Shift snaps the angle to 15°.

**The gradient editor** (the gradient tool's Edit…, the gradient map, gradient fill layers) works as Photoshop's:

- Colour stops under the strip, opacity stops over it: click a lane to add one where the gradient already is, drag a marker to move it, or off the strip to remove it.
- Between each pair of stops, a diamond: the midpoint, where the two are mixed half and half. Drag it, or set it below.
- A colour stop of your own gradients can follow the brush's primary or secondary colour.
- The gradient map and fill layers can start from any of your gradients or the presets (Gradients ▾) and save theirs to your gradients.
