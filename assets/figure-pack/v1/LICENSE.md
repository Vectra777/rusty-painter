# Realistic figures: licence and credits

The models in this folder (`male.glb`, `female.glb`, `child.glb`,
`hand-left.glb`, `hand-right.glb`) are made entirely from the assets of
**MakeHuman** as shipped with **MPFB 2**, the MakeHuman add-on for Blender:
its base mesh, its modelling targets (the body-shape morphs), its
`game_engine` skeleton and that skeleton's skin weights.

Those assets are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain
dedication) by the MakeHuman team, and so are these models: you may copy,
change and share them, for any purpose, without asking.

  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md
- The CC0 text MPFB ships with its assets:
  https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

  Commons, CC0"): https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html
- CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Built from MPFB 2 commit `9de0707fdc88438d7c53a1a2ac7937d9221e2431`
(version 2.0.18) with Blender 5.2 by `scripts/make_figure_pack.sh` (which
